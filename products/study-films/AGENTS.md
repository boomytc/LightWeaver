# study-films

Remotion renderer and optional capture adapter. Task instances live
under repo-root `data/` (`first-party/` / `projects/`), not in this
product. `public/projects/<id>` is a Remotion symlink into `data/`.

## Rules

- Films are `study-explainer` projects. Compositions are generated by `weaver sync`.
- Capture writes stills into that film's `assets/stills/<locale>/` when
  the film has an adapter.
- Render writes mp4 only to that film's `assets/outputs/`. Do not keep
  copies in this package's `out/` or `projects/`.
- Brand and copy come from `film.json`.
- `scripts/tts.py` is VoxCPM2. `scripts/asr.py` is Qwen3-ASR-0.6B GGUF for clone-source text (override with `asr_model` for 1.7B). Do not import other-repo explore trees.
- Comments and operator logs: Chinese. Identifiers: English.
