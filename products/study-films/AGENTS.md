# study-films

Remotion renderer. Task instances live under repo-root `data/`
(`first-party/` / `projects/`), not in this product. `public/projects/<id>`
is a Remotion symlink into `data/`. Job scripts live in `weaver/scripts`.

## Rules

- Films are `study-explainer` projects. Compositions are generated by `weaver sync`.
  `footage-narration` does not render here; `weaver render` sends those to ffmpeg compose.
- Render writes mp4 only to that film's `assets/outputs/`. Do not keep
  copies in this package's `out/` or `projects/`.
- Brand and copy come from `film.json`.
- Comments and operator logs: Chinese. Identifiers: English.
